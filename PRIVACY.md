# RevisitLoop Privacy Policy

**Effective date: September 4, 2026**

RevisitLoop is designed to be local-first. The extension stores the information it needs inside your browser and does not currently use a backend server, cloud account, analytics service, advertising service, or external database.

## Information RevisitLoop stores

When you create a Loop, RevisitLoop may store:

- the title of the page;
- the page URL;
- the reason or purpose you entered for saving the page;
- the lease option you selected;
- the lease expiration time, when applicable;
- whether a return-to-site reminder is enabled;
- the current status of the Loop;
- the time the Loop was created.

RevisitLoop also stores extension settings such as:

- theme preference;
- 12-hour or 24-hour time preference;
- whether system notifications are enabled;
- whether the first-run onboarding process has been completed.

This information is stored using your browser's local extension storage.

## Data transmission

RevisitLoop does not currently transmit your saved Loops, browsing information, settings, or other extension data to the developer or to third-party servers.

There is currently:

- no RevisitLoop account system;
- no cloud synchronization;
- no analytics or tracking service;
- no advertising;
- no sale of user data;
- no sharing of user data with third parties.

## Browser permissions

RevisitLoop uses browser permissions only for features provided by the extension.

### `activeTab`

Used when creating a Loop from the page you are currently viewing.

### `tabs`

Used to work with browser tabs, including opening saved pages, closing a tab after starting a Loop, and detecting when you return to a site for an enabled return-to-site reminder.

### `storage`

Used to save Loops and RevisitLoop settings locally in the browser.

### `alarms`

Used to schedule lease expiration so a Loop can move into Needs Attention without requiring the extension popup to remain open.

### `notifications`

Used to display optional system notifications when notifications are enabled in RevisitLoop Settings.

System notifications are turned off by default.

## Website access

RevisitLoop does not request a broad `<all_urls>` permission and does not inject a content script into every webpage.

Return-to-site reminders are handled using browser tab information and the hostname saved with a Loop.

## System notifications

If you enable system notifications, RevisitLoop may include information from a Loop, such as its purpose, in a notification displayed by your browser or operating system.

This does not send the Loop to a RevisitLoop server.

## Data retention and deletion

Loops remain in local browser storage until they are deleted, the extension's stored data is cleared, or the extension is removed.

You can also delete individual Archived Loops from inside RevisitLoop.

Removing RevisitLoop or clearing its extension storage may permanently remove your saved Loops and settings.

## Children

RevisitLoop is a general-purpose browser productivity tool and is not specifically directed toward children.

## Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated if RevisitLoop's features or data practices change.

If a future version introduces features such as accounts, cloud synchronization, analytics, or other external services, this policy will be updated before those features are released.
